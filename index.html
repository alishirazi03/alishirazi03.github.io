About Me

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Biosciences (134 credit hours, Feb 2022 – Feb 2026) at COMSATS University Islamabad. My academic training began at the bench with core molecular biology protocols and progressively expanded into computational genomics, sequence assembly, and biostatistical analysis.

For my 12-credit undergraduate thesis (awarded Grade A), I designed and executed a 104-participant case-control study (75 cholelithiasis patients and 29 controls) investigating the ATP8B1 gene variant rs766737165 (T>A) in the Pakistani population. Conducting genomic DNA extraction, Primer3/BLAST primer validation, TETRA-ARMS PCR, and statistical modeling in R and Excel deepened my interest in population genetics and membrane-transporter biology.

Alongside wet-lab research, I engineered an end-to-end bioinformatics workflow analyzing 100 SARS-CoV-2 genomes, integrating de Bruijn graph assembly, functional ORF annotation, multiple sequence alignment, and molecular-clock dating. I am currently preparing applications for European Master's programs in Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, and Molecular Biosciences.

Research Projects & Pipelines

Computational Genomics · Viral Evolution

SARS-CoV-2 Genomic Analysis Pipeline

End-to-End Reproducible Workflow Across 100 Viral Genomes & Reference Assembly

Jan 2026 – Mar 2026

Developed a reproducible bioinformatics pipeline analyzing 100 real-world SARS-CoV-2 pandemic genomes alongside de novo reconstruction of the complete 29,903 bp viral genome. Integrated quality control, functional ORF annotation, multiple sequence alignment, molecular-clock dating, and metabolic host-pathogen target identification.

Computational Pipeline Architecture
Raw Illumina Reads → SPAdes (De Novo) → QUAST (N50: 29,903 bp) → Prokka + Primer3 → MUSCLE / ViralMSA (n=100) → FastTree + LSD2 → BioCyc Target ID
  • De Novo Genome Assembly & QC: Reconstructed the full-length viral genome from raw sequencing reads using SPAdes and evaluated contiguity and completeness in QUAST (N50: 29,903 bp).
  • Functional Annotation & Diagnostics: Identified 11 major open reading frames (ORFs) via Prokka and designed diagnostic RT-PCR primer sets using Primer3 and NCBI BLAST.
  • Comparative Genomics (100 Genomes): Performed multiple sequence alignment across 100 isolates using MUSCLE and ViralMSA to evaluate mutation spectra and confirm zoonotic evolutionary origin.
  • Phylogenetics & Molecular Clock Dating: Constructed maximum-likelihood trees with FastTree and Taxonium, applying LSD2 molecular-clock models to estimate the time to most recent common ancestor (tMRCA: Oct–Nov 2019).
  • Antiviral Target Identification: Mapped purine metabolism pathways in BioCyc, identifying guanylate kinase as a candidate therapeutic target.

Molecular Genetics · Undergraduate Thesis (12 Credits, Grade A)

Assessment of the Role of ATP8B1 Gene in Gallstone Disease in the Pakistani Population

104-Participant Case-Control Genotyping Study of SNP rs766737165 (T>A)

Sep 2025 – Feb 2026

Designed and executed an experimental case-control study investigating the association between the ATP8B1 gene variant rs766737165 (T>A) and gallstone disease (cholelithiasis) in 104 participants (75 clinically confirmed patients and 29 healthy controls). Examined the role of this P-type ATPase aminophospholipid flippase in bile acid secretion and hepatobiliary lipid homeostasis.

Experimental & Analytical Workflow
Clinical Cohort (75 Cases / 29 Controls) → Phenol-Chloroform DNA Extraction → Primer3 + BLAST Design → TETRA-ARMS PCR & Gel Genotyping → Chi-Square & HWE (R / Excel)
  • Genomic DNA Isolation & QC: Extracted high-molecular-weight genomic DNA from 104 peripheral blood samples using the organic phenol-chloroform protocol and verified quality via agarose gel electrophoresis and spectrophotometry.
  • Allele-Specific Primer Design: Engineered inner and outer primer pairs targeting ATP8B1 SNP rs766737165 (T>A) in Primer3 and validated target specificity using NCBI BLAST.
  • TETRA-ARMS PCR Genotyping: Optimized thermal cycling parameters and resolved allele-specific amplicons on agarose gels across all 75 cases and 29 controls.
  • Biostatistical & Population Modeling: Evaluated genotype and allele frequency distributions using Chi-square contingency tests and Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium modeling in R and Excel.
  • Population Genetics Interpretation: Identified a monomorphic distribution for rs766737165 in the sampled cohort, establishing baseline local population data and formulating follow-up strategies for broader multi-exon screening.
